The paperwork you should be handed

Building work comes with certificates, and a buyer’s solicitor will ask for every one of them when you sell. Pick a job to see what you should end up holding.

  • Planning approval, or proof you didn’t need itEither the council’s permission or a lawful development certificate saying the extension was allowed without it.
  • Building Regulations completion certificateIssued by building control after they have inspected the finished work. It is the first thing a solicitor asks for.
  • Structural calculationsThe engineer’s figures for any steel beam, kept with the drawings.
