Stuart’s advice before the cold sets in

“You wouldn’t leave your car for 6 months and then expect it to run perfectly.”

  1. Give your heating a good run before it turns really cold. It shows up a sticking valve or a burnt out motor before you need the heat.
  2. When it freezes, check the condensate pipe outside your boiler, and lag it if it isn’t already. A frozen pipe makes the boiler gurgle before it stops with a fault code.
  3. Any problems, call straight away on 07748 604652, rather than waiting for it to fail.
