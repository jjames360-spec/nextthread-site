Do this straight away

Water is leaking

  1. Turn the water off at the stopcock. It’s usually under the kitchen sink. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Open the cold taps to drain what’s left in the pipes.
  3. If water is near lights or sockets, switch the electrics off at the fuse box. Don’t touch anything wet.
  4. Put a bucket under it, then text Martin.
