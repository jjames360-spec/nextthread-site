Your drain, or the water company’s?

It depends where the problem is. As a rule:

  • Yours. The drain inside your boundary that serves only your home. That’s the work Lewis does.
  • Usually the water company’s. Drains you share with neighbours, and the pipe once it has left your boundary.

Not sure which you’ve got? Message Lewis a photo and ask.
