Questions people ask

Do I need to write anything?

No. Send photos and tell me what you do in a message or a voice note. I write it, you read it, we change anything you don't like.

Who owns the website?

You do. The web address is registered in your name and the files are yours. If you ever want to move it somewhere else, I hand everything over.

I've already got a website. Can you fix it?

I'll rebuild it properly and move your existing web address across, so the old one keeps working right up until the new one takes over. Nothing goes offline.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer, once you've seen the preview and said yes. The monthly plan is a standing order you can stop any month.

Is £595 really everything?

For the site, your web address and your first year's hosting, yes. After the first year you choose: £60 a year for hosting alone, £25 a month if you'd like me to keep it updated as well (new photos, reviews, prices), or it's all included in the monthly plan. Or take the site elsewhere, and I'll help you move it.

Where are you based?

The West Midlands, but I build sites for tradespeople anywhere in the UK. Everything happens over WhatsApp and email, so it makes no difference where you are.
