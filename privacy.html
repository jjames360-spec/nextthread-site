Privacy notice

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version: I keep the details you give me so I can reply to you and build your site, I don't sell them to anyone, and this website sets no cookies.

Who I am

NextThread is run by Joshua James, a sole trader based in the West Midlands. For anything about your personal information, email joshua@nextthreadautomation.co.uk or WhatsApp 07856 075882. I am the "controller" of the information described below.

What I collect, and why

When you get in touch (by WhatsApp, text, email or phone): your name, your number or email address, your business name and whatever you tell me in your message. I use this to reply to you, send you a preview, and arrange the work. The legal basis is that it's necessary to take the steps you've asked for before entering a contract.

When you become a client: the above, plus the material you send me for your website (photos, reviews, descriptions of your business), your payment details as they appear on bank statements, and the web address registered in your name. I use this to build and host your site, provide the monthly plan if you take it, and keep the records a business has to keep. The legal basis is performance of our contract and, for the records, my legal obligations.

If I contact you first: I sometimes approach tradespeople whose business details are publicly listed, to offer a website. I'll always say who I am, and if you reply STOP or tell me you're not interested, I'll make a note and won't contact you again. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in promoting my business, and you can object at any time.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell or rent your details to anyone.
  • I don't use your details for anything unrelated to what you asked for.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The "make it yours" tool on the homepage runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere unless you tap the WhatsApp button.

Who else sees your information

Only the services I use to run the business, each of which processes data on my instructions: WhatsApp (Meta) for messages, Google Workspace for email and files, GitHub for hosting this website, and the registrar and hosting providers used for client websites. If you take the monthly plan, the missed-call and follow-up texts are sent through a messaging provider using your business's details. Some of these providers are outside the UK; where they are, the transfer relies on the UK's adequacy regulations or the provider's standard contractual clauses.

This page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. No other third party is contacted by this site.

If you take the monthly plan

The plan handles messages to and from your customers. For that information you are the controller and I am your processor: I only use it to provide the service you've asked for, I keep it secure, I don't use it for my own purposes, and I delete it when the plan ends unless you ask me to hand it over. You're responsible for making sure your own customers know you use a text-back service.

How long I keep things

  • Enquiries that don't go anywhere: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client records, invoices and correspondence about the work: 6 years, because tax law requires business records to be kept.
  • Website material (photos, text): for as long as I host your site, then handed over or deleted.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, ask me to stop using it, or ask for a copy in a portable form. Email me and I'll respond within a month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though I'd rather you gave me the chance to put it right first.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version goes here with a new date. Nothing changes retrospectively.
